🏛️ Government Schemes for Farmers

Tap a scheme to see full details, eligibility, and how to apply.

💰 PM-KISAN Income Support

Benefit: ₹6,000 per year, paid in three ₹2,000 installments directly to your bank account.

Eligibility: Any farmer family with cultivable land (income-tax payers and government officials excluded).

How to apply: Register at the portal (Farmers Corner → New Farmer Registration) or your nearest CSC.

Visit Official Portal ↗
🛡️ Crop Insurance (PMFBY) Insurance

Benefit: Low-premium insurance covering crop losses from drought, flood, pests, and unseasonal rain.

Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops, including loanee and non-loanee farmers.

How to apply: Report loss within 72 hours on the portal or through your bank.

Visit Official Portal ↗
🧪 Soil Health Card Soil Testing

Benefit: Free soil report with nutrient status, pH, and crop-specific fertilizer advice.

Eligibility: All farmers with cultivable land.

How to apply: Register with Aadhaar on the portal, or submit a sample at a soil testing lab / CSC.

Visit Official Portal ↗
📈 eNAM Market Selling

Benefit: National online market to sell produce directly to buyers across India for better prices.

Eligibility: Farmers, traders, and FPOs registered with a linked APMC mandi.

How to apply: Register your details and link your local APMC mandi on the portal.

Visit Official Portal ↗
💳 Kisan Credit Card Credit

Benefit: Farm loans up to ₹3 lakh at low interest (around 4% with timely repayment).

Eligibility: Farmers, tenant farmers, sharecroppers, and self-help groups.

How to apply: Apply online through the government credit portal or your bank.

Visit Official Portal ↗
👴 PM Kisan Maandhan Pension

Benefit: Pension of ₹3,000 per month after age 60 for small and marginal farmers.

Eligibility: Small/marginal farmers aged 18–40 with up to 2 hectares of land.

How to apply: Enrol with Aadhaar and bank details on the portal or at a CSC.

Visit Official Portal ↗
☀️ PM-KUSUM Solar Energy

Benefit: Subsidy support for solar pumps and grid-connected solar power for farmers.

Eligibility: Individual farmers, groups, cooperatives, and panchayats using agricultural pumps.

How to apply: Apply through the state renewable energy department or designated DISCOM channel.

Visit Official Portal ↗
🌾 National Horticulture Mission Horticulture

Benefit: Financial assistance for protected cultivation, quality planting material, and post-harvest infrastructure.

Eligibility: Farmers, growers, and FPOs engaged in horticulture crops.

How to apply: Reach the state horticulture department, district agriculture office, or local nodal agency.

Visit Official Portal ↗
🦐 Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana Aquaculture

Benefit: Support for fish farmers, hatcheries, cold chains, and aquaculture infrastructure.

Eligibility: Fishers, fish farmer producer organizations, and aquaculture entrepreneurs.

How to apply: Apply through the state fisheries department or the PMMSY portal.

Visit Official Portal ↗
